Ido ↔ Esperanto

Neural translation that runs entirely in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Model: komapc/nllb-ido-epo-600m (NLLB-200 600M fine-tuned for Ido). For comparison, see the rule-based translator at tradukilo.lipu.dev.

Ido Esperanto
The model is downloaded once and then cached by your browser.
It was trained on short sentences, so long text is translated one sentence at a time. This build has a reduced vocabulary and int8 weights (chrF 73.0 vs 73.4 for the full model on the held-out test set). Model license: CC-BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). Training data includes Tatoeba (CC-BY 2.0 FR). Code: komapc/ido-epo-llm.
